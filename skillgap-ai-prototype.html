Why it starts with HR discovery

HR shouldn't need to know which skills their team is missing before running an assessment — that's the whole point of the tool.

1
NEW

HR describes their team's problems

HR answers plain-language questions: what challenges is your team facing? What's slowing them down? What's changing in your company?

2
NEW

AI maps problems → relevant skills

AI reasons over the answers and recommends which skills to actually assess — e.g. "Your team is adopting AI tools but you have no training → assess AI literacy."

3

Employees self-assess with root cause follow-ups

Low scores trigger adaptive questions: Is the gap about training? Tool access? Lack of practice time? Confidence?

4

HR gets precise, targeted interventions

Not "run a course for everyone." Instead: specific actions per root cause cluster — so every training dollar lands on the right problem.

The difference it makes

Same team, same score — completely different interventions depending on whether you asked why.
